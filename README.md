# ExpressiveAI: How I Built Th[AI]

Th[AI], a play on my name, Thai, gives portfolio visitors a way to ask about my work, projects, and education.
I call the idea ExpressiveAI: bringing personality into AI through art, expression, and movement.

[Visit my portfolio](https://nghiemthai1.github.io/) and open Ask Th[AI].

## Designing the character

I used ChatGPT and Gemini to explore concepts, then worked with Codex to build and refine the experience.
I chose the personality, shaped the interaction, and made the final product decisions.

The early design board explored three views and five expressions: smiling, thinking, explaining, excitement, and listening.
A gray blazer, blue shirt, laptop, coffee mug, and plant established a friendly workspace.
The guiding phrase was "Curiosity today. Progress tomorrow."

![Early character design board with views, expressions, desk accessories, and colors](assets/images/readme/characterboard.jpg)

I also tried a rigged 3D character in GLB format, but the result felt generic and unfriendly.
Illustration better expressed the warmth I wanted: someone sitting across a desk, ready for a conversation.

## Bringing Th[AI] to the website

The dark background, gold accents, and compact Ask Th[AI] launcher connect the assistant to the portfolio.
Resting poses, shifts in attention, and subtle movement give the character a visible response before the words arrive.

I refined speech bubbles, character scale, and controls across laptop and phone screens.
Both use the same illustrated character, with placement adjusted for the available space.

## Keeping answers honest

Th[AI] answers from my public professional background.
The interface identifies the assistant as AI and acknowledges possible mistakes.
AI responses are not personal promises from me.
Questions requiring my judgment belong in a real conversation.

ExpressiveAI reflects how I want to work with AI: human intent guiding the tools toward something personal and useful.
